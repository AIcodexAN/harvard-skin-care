SECTION 4: SKIN PROTECTION: LOTIONS, POTIONS, AND MORE
Reduce sun exposure.
Remember those three words—they're the key to protecting your skin and combatting the effects of photoaging. This section addresses sun and other causes of skin damage, and then gets into lotions, creams, cosmeceuticals, and other products you likely have tried, or at least thought about trying.

EXPLORING COSMECEUTICALS
The U.S. Food and Drug Administration says the word "cosmeceutical" has no meaning under the law.
But in the cosmetic industry, it’s become common—a word referring to cosmetic products that have medicinal or drug-like benefits. And Merriam-Webster, which traces the known use of “cosmeceutical” to 1985, defines it as a “preparation that has pharmaceutical properties.” Click on the “Play Audio” arrow above each photo in our slideshow to learn more.

[第 4 张] GROWTH FACTORS
If you’ve heard the phrase “vampire facial,” you may know it to be an application of platelet-rich plasma. It’s one way that so-called “growth factors” are used to help skin. Click “Play Audio.”

[第 5 张] PEPTIDES
These compounds, which consist of two or more amino acids linked in a chain, have various roles in the body. For example, there’s evidence that the peptide DMAE (that’s short for dimethylaminoethanol) may smooth out skin and decrease wrinkle depth. Click on “Play Audio.”

BIRTHMARKS
Birthmarks are areas of discolored or raised skin
seen at birth or within a few weeks of birth. They may be made up of malformed pigment cells or blood vessels. Medical experts don’t know what causes birthmarks, but most are benign (not cancerous) and don't require treatment.

[第 5 张] EXPLAINING ULTRAVIOLET RAYS
There are three main wavelengths coming from the sun:
• UVA is the longest wavelength—and the one that penetrates your skin most deeply.
• UVB is the next-longest wavelength.
• UVC, the shortest wavelength, is normally blocked by the atmosphere's ozone layer.

SECTION 1 • Getting Started
Along with
Dr. Mallika Marshall's introduction,
this opening section includes lessons on skin basics, layer-by-layer, and a special slideshow presentation on how the aging process affects your skin.

SECTION 2 • Common Skin Conditions
You'll learn in this section
about the types of skin conditions most commonly experienced:
• Adult acne
• Athlete's foot
• Birthmarks
• Cellulitis
• Contact dermatitis
• Dry skin
• Eczema
• Eyelash renewal
• Excess hair growth
• Hair loss
• Melasma
• Psoriasis
• Rosacea
• Shingles
• Skin conditions experienced by people of color
• Toenail fungus
• Vitiligo
• Warts
You'll also find a lesson on the most common skin conditions for people of color.

SECTION 3 • Skin Cancer
The lessons
in this section include a discussion of various types of skin cancer:
• Actinic keratoses
• Basal cell and squamous cell carcinomas
• Melanoma
Importantly, you'll also learn how to identify trouble spots and what to do about them.

SECTION 4 • Skin Protection: Lotions, Potions, and More
W
hat can you do
to keep yourself free of skin problems and conditions? Plenty, as you'll learn in this section. You'll find lessons on sun and sun protection, multiple causes of skin damage, and how to do a skin self-exam. We also provide a lesson describing a three-step daily skin care routine.
Besides learning how to protect your skin,
you'll find in this section some important lessons on moisturizers, exfoliants, and cosmeceuticals: Are they effective in enhancing our skin's health, and can they "repair" skin issues? Find out ahead.

ABOUT OUR "NOTES" FEATURE
Use our special Notes area
to store material you want to emphasize, remember, or research. Your Notes area is viewable only by you. Here's how it works:
Highlight a passage of text on a given page.
Click on the "Notes" link near the bottom of the left-side column.
Paste the information into the box that opens, and then click "Save." The material is automatically filed into the appropriate lesson.
When you click into the Notes section (the link appears in a red bar to your left), you can add to or remove your archived material as needed.

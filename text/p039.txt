QUICK GUIDE TO PRODUCT INGREDIENTS
The labels on anti-aging products
promote some impressive-sounding ingredients. But what are they? Can they help your skin? Are there side effects? This glossary defines many of the terms or ingredient names you're likely to encounter. Click on the "Expand " button in each box to learn more.
PaulynnC / iStock / Getty Images Plus
REGULATIONS VOID
Keep in mind
that most cosmetic products are not regulated, and in most cases there is little or no published evidence to substantiate their effectiveness.
PaulynnC / iStock / Getty Images Plus
WHAT'S IN SKIN-CARE PRODUCTS?
Expand
Alpha lipoic acid
Alpha lipoic acid
is an antioxidant claimed to have a modest effect in decreasing skin roughness and wrinkles.
Expand
Antioxidants
Antioxidants
are substances that neutralize free radicals, damaging molecules that accelerate cellular aging and promote cancer.
Expand
Coenzyme Q10 (ubiquinone)
Coenzyme Q
10
(ubiquinone)
is an antioxidant that protects against UVA and may modestly reduce wrinkle depth.
Expand
Copper peptide
Copper
is a metal with antioxidant properties found in every cell in the human body.
Copper peptides
enhance wound healing and may modestly increase collagen and elastin production.
Expand
Dimethylaminoethanol (DMAE)
Dimethylaminoethanol (DMAE)
is a neurotransmitter produced in the brain; an extract in gel form is purported to reduce wrinkles, neck sagging, and circles under the eyes.
Expand
Genistein
Genistein
is a derivative of soy and an antioxidant. It inhibits UVB damage to the skin.
Expand
Green tea
Green tea
is an antioxidant and an anti-inflammatory agent. It may inhibit UV damage and photoaging.
Expand
Growth factors
Growth factors
are substances that occur naturally in the human body and in plants. They contribute to wound healing and may repair photodamaged skin.
Expand
Hyaluronic acid
Hyaluronic acid
is a naturally occurring sugar that retains moisture and adds volume to skin. See our lesson on hyaluronic acid
by clicking here
.
Expand
Kinetin (N6-furfuryladenine)
Kinetin (N6-furfuryladenine)
is a plant growth factor and an antioxidant claimed to reduce wrinkles, smooth skin texture, and even out skin tone.
Expand
Niacinamide (vitamin B3)
Niacinamide (vitamin B
3
)
is an antioxidant that is promoted to help reverse signs of photoaging.
Expand
Peptides
Peptides
are short-chain amino acids that may assist with production of collagen and elastin or have other beneficial effects on the skin. (For more on peptides, see Slide #5 in our slideshow presentation "Exploring Cosmeceuticals"
by clicking here
.)
Expand
Retinoid
A retinoid
is any of several derivatives of vitamin A that have been shown to reduce photodamage and increase collagen production. See our lesson on retinoids
by clicking here
.
Expand
Vitamin C (ascorbic acid)
Vitamin C (ascorbic acid)
, when used in topical preparations, is thought to reduce wrinkles and improve skin texture and tone.
Expand
Vitamin E
Vitamin E
is an antioxidant vitamin; limited research suggests some protective effects for the skin.

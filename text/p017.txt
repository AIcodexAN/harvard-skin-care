KEY LESSON
Respect the sun's rays
and understand the effects of photoaging, as explained in the slideshow presentation
"The Aging Process"
in Section 1. Protecting yourself is simpler than you may think—from remembering to use sunscreen to wearing wide-brimmed hats and long sleeves.

How to Quit Smoking
(NEARLY) INSTANT GRATIFICATION
If you or a loved one
don't see the benefits of smoking, read through this list. Your body begins to experience the benefits of quitting almost instantly.
Source: Quit Smoking for Good (Harvard Medical School Guide)
YOUR BODY'S REWARDS FOR SMOKING CESSATION
Expand
AFTER TWO HOURS...
After two hours
without a cigarette, nicotine begins to leave your system.
© Paulus Rusyanto | Dreamstime.com
Expand
AFTER 12 HOURS...
After 12 hours
, the carbon monoxide has completely left your system. Your blood now carries oxygen more efficiently.
© Vampy1 | Dreamstime.com
Expand
WITHIN ONE WEEK...
Within one week
, your senses of taste and smell sharpen. Your breath, hair, fingers, and teeth are cleaner. Your circulation improves. All the nicotine is gone from your system, along with the worst of the withdrawal symptoms, in most cases.
© Kim Reinick | Dreamstime.com
Expand
WITHIN ONE MONTH...
Within one month,
the cilia (the hair-like structures that line your airways) begin to recover and remove more mucus from your system. Sinus congestion, fatigue, and shortness of breath also decrease.
© Sebastian Kaulitzki | Dreamstime.com
Expand
AFTER ONE YEAR...
After one year
, your risk of having a heart attack or stroke is reduced, and it continues to fall substantially over the first two to four years. The risk of lung cancer also falls, but more slowly. Your risk of heart disease is half that of a smoker.
© Syda Productions | Dreamstime.com
Expand
AFTER FIVE YEARS...
After five years,
your risk of stroke may be the same as that of a nonsmoker.
© Fizkes | Dreamstime.com
Expand
AFTER 10 AND 15 YEARS...
After 10 years
, your risk of lung cancer death is cut in half compared with a smoker’s. And
after 15 years
, your risk of heart disease is the same as a nonsmoker’s.
© Seanlockephotography | Dreamstime.com
